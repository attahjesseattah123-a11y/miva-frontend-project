A guided process

How support is planned to work

  1. Step 1

    Describe the problem

    Tell us what you were trying to do and what happened. Include any message your phone displayed.

  2. Step 2

    Share relevant phone details

    Provide the phone brand and model, plus a way to contact you if the service is available.

  3. Step 3

    Receive guided troubleshooting

    Once the service is operational, JPS can guide you through steps and explain what they do.
