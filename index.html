How it works

This is the planned support approach. The online support request process is not available yet.

  1. Step 1

    Describe the phone problem

    Explain what you were trying to do and what happened.

  2. Step 2

    Follow clear guidance

    Work through manageable steps with an explanation along the way.

  3. Step 3

    Understand what changed

    Learn what the steps did so you can feel more confident using your device.
